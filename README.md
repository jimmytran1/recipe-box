# Recipe Box — how to use it

A personal recipe collection you can talk to. Save recipes from anywhere, then ask cooking questions and they get answered from your own saved recipes.

## Setup (one time)
1. Connect a folder in Cowork (any folder you want your recipes kept in).
2. Say "save this recipe" with a link, screenshot, website URL, or pasted text. A `recipes/` folder is created automatically, with one clean file per recipe plus an `_index.md` list.

## Adding recipes
Just paste or drop in any of these and say "add to my recipe box":
- A recipe website link
- A TikTok / video link (paste the caption or steps if the link alone has no text — video can't be watched directly)
- A screenshot or photo of a recipe
- Typed or pasted text

Each recipe is saved in a standard format (see `recipe-template.md`) with servings, measurements, tags, and the original source link.

## Asking questions
Once recipes are saved, ask things like:
- "How much garlic in the shrimp pasta?" (measurements)
- "Scale the lasagna to 8 people." (scaling)
- "I'm out of buttermilk — what can I use?" (substitutions)
- "What can I make with chicken and rice?" (search by ingredient)
- "Plan 4 dinners this week and give me a grocery list." (meal planning)

## Sharing it
The skill is self-contained — anyone with Cowork can install it, connect their own folder, and their recipes stay in their own `recipes/` folder. Nothing is tied to one account.
